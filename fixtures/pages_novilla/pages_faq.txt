Do I have to pay for returning my Novilla products?
Of course No! If you are in the United States, we offer free shipping and returns on our products within trial period. So you can return your products for free and get your money back within 5 business days after we receive the product(s).
Do I need to wait for 72 hours before I use my new mattress?
Though it is possible to use your mattress after six hours, we suggest that you wait for the recommended 72 hours. Doing so will allow your mattress to fully expand and eliminate any unpleasant odors that may be produced during manufacturing and transportation.
Will your mattress topper make my mattress sleep hotter?
Definitely no! Novilla toppers are designed for a ultra soft, cloud-like cooling sleep. To ensure a cool, comfort sleep environment, our topper chose gel infused memory foam and egg cratefoam to keep optimal airflow. Learn more about Novilla toppers.
Are Novilla products safe?

Explanation of the Mattress Recycling Fee.
By existing U.S. laws, the mattress industry is required to establish mattress recycling programs in certain states. Entities, including but not limited to physical retailers, online sellers, and companies that sell mattresses to commercial end-users (such as hotels, lodging facilities, boarding schools, colleges and universities, nursing homes, medical facilities, etc.), are obligated to collect mattress recycling fees from residents of these states.These entities must remit all collected recycling fees in full to the Mattress Recycling Council (MRC) via MRCreporting.org.For further details regarding recycling fees, please click the link.
