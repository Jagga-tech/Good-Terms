FAQs about Black Friday
Do I need a coupon or promo code at checkout?
No worries! Most discounts are applied automatically at checkout. If a special offer requires a code, we’ll make it clear.
How can I get an extra 10% off?
Simply add any 2 eligible items to your cart to enjoy an extra 10% off.
Can I apply the mattress sale discount to a previous order?
Unfortunately, discounts are only available for purchases made during the current mattress sale.
Are the product discounts applied before or after tax?
All discounts are applied before taxes and exclude the Worry-Free Purchase Protection Fee. Taxes and applicable fees are calculated separately at checkout.
Can I combine multiple discounts or promo codes?
No. Discounts and promotional offers cannot be combined. The best available discount will be automatically applied to your order.
Which products are eligible for Flash Sale deals?
Flash Sale offers are available on selected products and sizes only. The final price may vary depending on the size you select. Please refer to the price displayed at checkout for the exact discount.
