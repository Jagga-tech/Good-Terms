What is the 100 night sleep trial?
Our 100-night trial ensures you love your rest. We recommend a 30-night adjustment period, but if it's not a perfect fit, we offer free returns and full refunds for both unopened and used mattresses. Simply contact us to arrange a free local pickup; once confirmed, your refund will be processed within 5 business days.
Do you offer financing?
Yes! We offer flexible financing to help you rest now and pay later. At checkout, you can choose from Afterpay, Affirm, or Klarna for easy monthly or interest-free payment options. We also support HSA/FSA accounts for eligible health savings purchases related to sleep wellness.
How long will it take to receive my order?
Once your order is processed, you will receive a tracking number within 1–2 business days, and your package will be delivered within 3–7 business days via our trusted carriers (FedEx/UPS/GOFO). We work to ship all orders promptly to ensure you receive your mattress as quickly as possible.
How long should I wait before sleeping on my new mattress?
We recommend giving your new mattress 24–72 hours to fully expand. While it's okay to sleep on it after about 6 hours, allowing a little extra time helps the mattress reach its full shape and lets any temporary packaging odors naturally fade away.
How long does the mattress warranty last?
Every mattress comes with a 10-Year Limited Warranty, covering manufacturing defects such as abnormal sagging, structural foam breakdown, or issues related to workmanship. If something goes wrong that shouldn’t happen with normal use, we’re here to help make it right.
What makes the Bliss™ Memory Foam Mattress a good value memory foam mattress?
The Bliss™ Memory Foam Mattress is designed as a best-value memory foam mattress that focuses on balance rather than extremes. Instead of feeling too soft or too firm, its layered foam construction helps distribute body weight evenly while still giving you that gentle, comforting 'sink-in' feel people love from memory foam.
Is the Bliss™ Memory Foam Mattress good for back pain and spinal support?
This is exactly where the Bliss™ Memory Foam Mattress feels most reassuring. The stable high-density foam base works quietly under your body to support natural spinal alignment, while the top layers ease pressure around sensitive areas like the lower back and hips. Instead of feeling forced into a position, your body feels gently guided into a more relaxed, supported posture that helps reduce that stiff, heavy feeling in the morning.
Does the Bliss™ Memory Foam Mattress sleep cool for hot sleepers?
If you're someone who struggles with night sweats, the Bliss™ Memory Foam Mattress is designed to feel more breathable than traditional memory foam mattresses. The NanoGEL™ Cooling Memory Foam with 30% more cooling particles helps draw heat away from your body, so the sleep surface feels more consistently cool and comfortable.
What does the Bliss™ Memory Foam Mattress feel like — soft mattress or firm mattress?
It’s right in that 'sweet spot' many people look for in a memory foam mattress. The surface feels soft and inviting when you lie down, while the deeper layers gradually push back just enough to keep your body supported instead of sinking too deeply.
Is the Bliss™ Memory Foam Mattress good for side sleepers and combination sleepers?
Absolutely! Especially if you tend to switch positions during the night. The Bliss™ Memory Foam Mattress moves with you, gently contouring around your shoulders and hips when you're on your side, then naturally rebalancing support as you turn over. It's the kind of memory foam mattress comfort that doesn't get in your way. You simply stay comfortably supported, no matter how your sleep shifts through the night!
Does the Bliss™ Memory Foam Mattress help keep the sleep surface fresh and breathable?
One of the small but important details of the Bliss™ Memory Foam Mattress is its OEKO-TEX® certified breathable cover. It feels soft against the skin, supports airflow, and features a hidden zipper design so you can easily remove and wash it — helping your bed feel cleaner and fresher over time.
Will the Bliss™ Memory Foam Mattress sag or lose support over time?
The Bliss™ Memory Foam Mattress is reinforced with a high-density support foam base designed to maintain structure and reduce sagging. This helps the mattress stay stable and supportive over the long term, instead of developing uneven dips in commonly used areas.
Who is the Bliss™ Memory Foam Mattress best suited for?
The Bliss™ Memory Foam Mattress is ideal for people looking for a reliable everyday memory foam mattress that doesn't overcomplicate things. It works especially well for side sleepers, back sleepers, and anyone who wants a mix of pressure relief, cooling comfort, and stable support in one simple, well-balanced mattress.
Do I pay for shipping?
No! We offer free standard shipping to all customers in the contiguous United States with no hidden fees. Please note that remote or rural addresses may incur an additional delivery fee charged directly by our carriers (FedEx/UPS/GOFO) based on the distance to your location.
Can I delay the shipping time or delivery date of my order?
If your order has not yet been processed or paid for, our Customer Support team may be able to help you schedule a later shipping time.
Can I exchange a product if I'm not satisfied?
Eligible items may be exchanged once upon approval by our customer service team. Replacement items are subject to availability, and any price difference will be charged or refunded accordingly. Each eligible item is limited to one exchange.
What happens after I return my mattress?
Once your return is approved and the returned item meets our return requirements, your refund will be processed according to our Return Policy. Refund timing and the return process may vary depending on the product type and return method. Please refer to our Returns Policy for details.
What does the warranty actually cover?
The mattress warranty typically covers issues like visible indentations beyond normal use, material cracking, or defects in the mattress construction. It's designed to protect the long-term structure of your mattress so you can continue enjoying stable support year after year.
What is not covered under warranty?
Like most mattress warranties, this does not include normal softening over time, changes in comfort preference, or damage caused by improper use or an unsuitable base. We always recommend using a supportive bed frame to ensure the best long-term performance.
How do I file a warranty claim?
If something feels off with your mattress, simply reach out to our support team with your order details and photos of the issue. Our team will guide you step by step and help review your claim as quickly as possible.
How do HSA and FSA accounts work?
HSA and FSA accounts allow you to set aside pre-tax dollars to pay for qualified health-related expenses, helping you save on everyday healthcare costs. HSAs are typically paired with high-deductible health plans and allow unused funds to roll over from year to year, while FSAs are usually used within the same plan year. When applied to eligible Novilla sleep products, these pre-tax benefits can translate into meaningful savings on your purchase while making better sleep more accessible.
How does using HSA/FSA funds save me money?
Purchases made with HSA/FSA funds are tax-free, which means you're using pre-tax dollars instead of post-tax income. Depending on your tax bracket, this can effectively reduce your overall cost by up to 30% or more, making it a smart way to invest in better sleep while getting more value from every dollar you spend.
Why is a Health Assessment and Provider Review required?
Truemed includes a short health assessment reviewed by a licensed provider to determine eligibility. This step ensures that HSA/FSA usage follows IRS guidelines while connecting sleep health needs—like back pain or poor recovery—to appropriate product recommendations.
Can I use HSA/FSA funds to buy a Novilla mattress?
HSA/FSA eligibility is determined by healthcare guidelines, which means only the health-related portion of a mattress purchase may qualify for reimbursement. In simple terms, this can include advanced features designed to support sleep health and comfort, rather than the basic product cost. Truemed helps break this down clearly during the process so you always understand what qualifies before completing your order.
What if I don’t have enough HSA/FSA funds?
You can still complete your purchase using a regular credit or debit card at checkout. After that, you may submit the eligible portion of your order for reimbursement using your remaining HSA/FSA balance through Truemed. This way, you don’t have to delay your purchase, and you can still take full advantage of your available benefits.
What happens if my HSA/FSA payment is declined?
If a payment doesn't go through, it's usually related to eligibility or account balance limits. In that case, you can switch to a standard payment method or reach out to support for help completing your order.
