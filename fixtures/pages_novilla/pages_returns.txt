Return Policy
100-Night Mattress Sleep Trial
We believe everyone deserves good sleep — and a worry-free way to find it. Every Novilla mattress comes with our 100-Night Mattress Sleep Trial. If you're not sleeping better, we'll make it right at no cost to you.
What matters most: is your mattress still in the box, or already on your bed?
Step 1 — Warranty first. Visible dent (≥1 inch), cracks, splits, seam failure, or anything listed in our Warranty Policy? That's a warranty claim — we'll repair or replace at no cost.Step 2 — This page. No defect, but you're just not happy with the feel? Changed your mind? Within your trial window? You're in the right place. We'll resolve it below.
Still in the Box? — Free Returns
If your mattress is still in its original vacuum-sealed packaging, or has been opened but shows no signs of use and can be restored to its original packaging, returning it is simple:
Window: Within 30 days of delivery
What we do: Email you a free prepaid return label
What you do: Keep the mattress in its original vacuum-sealed packaging and drop it off at the carrier
Refund: 100% full refund once the return tracking shows the package is on its way back
This also applies to unopened pillows, toppers, and bed frames purchased from novillashop.com.
Tip: If you're unsure about your purchase, don't open the package right away. Once opened, the mattress expands and cannot be re-boxed — which means the return path changes (see below).
Already Sleeping on It? (Opened)
Once a mattress is unpacked, the foam expands to its full size and cannot be compressed back into the box. That's by design — it's what gives you the full sleep experience. Free Returns still apply — we just handle returns a little differently for opened mattresses. Here's how:
Step 1: Give It Time (30-Day Adjustment Period)
Your body needs time to adjust to a new sleep surface. Most people need about 4 weeks before their muscles and spine fully adapt. For this reason, we require a minimum 30-night trial before accepting a return request. If you contact us before the 30-day mark, we'll ask you to keep sleeping on it and reach back out if you're still not satisfied.
Step 2: Start Your Return
After 30 nights and within 100 days of delivery, if you're still not satisfied, contact our Customer Care Team. We'll help you with your return — it's simple and free. Please note, the mattress must be in sanitary, donatable condition — no rips, stains, or signs of excessive wear.
Topper — 30-Day Trial
Try your topper risk-free for 30 days. Toppers are vacuum-sealed — once opened, they expand to full size and cannot be re-boxed. Here's how returns work for each situation:
Still in the Box? — Free Returns
If your topper is still in its original vacuum-sealed packaging, or has been opened but shows no signs of use and can be restored to its original packaging:
Window: Within 30 days of delivery
What we do: Email you a free prepaid return label
What you do: Keep the topper in its original packaging and drop it off at the carrier
Refund: 100% full refund once the return tracking shows the package is on its way back
Already Opened It?
Within 30 days of delivery, if you're not happy with your topper, contact our Customer Care Team — it's simple and free. Please note, the topper must be in sanitary, donatable condition — no rips, stains, or signs of excessive wear.

How to Start a Claim
To initiate a return or resolution request, contact our Customer Care Team:
Email: support@novilla.us
Please have the following ready:
Order ID (from your confirmation email)
Your full name, phone number, and email address
Photos or videos of the product and, for mattresses, the law tag
A brief description of the issue
We respond to all requests within 24 business hours. Once a refund is approved, you'll see the money back in your account within 5–10 business days, depending on your bank or credit card provider.
Frequently Asked Questions
Q: What if I used financing (e.g., Affirm) to purchase?
Refunds are processed back to your financing account. If a partial refund is applied, the amount will be credited against your remaining balance.
Q: Can I return more than one mattress?
Our Sleep Trial is limited to one mattress per household per year. This helps us keep our prices fair for everyone.
Q: Can I exchange my item?
Eligible items may be exchanged once upon approval by our customer service team. Replacement items are subject to availability, and any price difference will be charged or refunded accordingly. Each eligible item is limited to one exchange.
Q: What if my mattress arrived damaged or defective?
That's a warranty matter, not a return. Contact us immediately with photos or videos, and we'll repair or replace it at no cost to you under our Warranty Policy.
Q: What if the return process doesn't work for my situation?
We hear you — and our Free Returns commitment doesn't change. Contact us and we'll work with you directly. Your satisfaction is our priority, and we'll make sure you're taken care of.
Terms & Conditions
The 100-Night Mattress Sleep Trial applies only to mattresses purchased directly from novillashop.com.
For products purchased through third-party retailers (Amazon, eBay, Walmart, etc.), please refer to that retailer's return policy. We're happy to assist if you encounter difficulties.
Clearance Items: All clearance items are considered final sale and are not eligible for returns, refunds, or exchanges due to a change of mind or personal preference. Please review the product details carefully before placing your order.
Products purchased from second-hand markets or unauthorized resellers are not covered.
