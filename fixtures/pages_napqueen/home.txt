What Goes Inside Our Mattress
Cooling Gel Memory Foam
Cooling Gel Memory Foam include improved comfort and support, as well as a cooler sleeping
surface.
Green Tea Memory Foam
The green tea extract is added for its antioxidant properties and ability to help neutralize
odors
Charcoal Memory Foam
Activated charcoal is a highly porous substance that is often used for its ability to absorb and
filter out impurities.
High Density Foam
High density foam is a type of foam that is made up of tiny cells that are closely packed
together
Bonnell Coil Springs
These mattresses give nice support to the body, hence preventing back pain also ensure air
circulation and provide maximum comfort
Pocket Coil
The fabric sleeves are glued together, allowing each spring to move independently within its
fabric
casing that means no motion transfer when sleeping with a partner.
