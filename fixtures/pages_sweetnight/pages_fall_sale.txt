SweetNight Fall Sale FAQ
How much can I save during the SweetNight Fall Sale?
During the SweetNight Fall Sale, you can save up to 40% sitewide.
You can also get up to an extra 20% off when you purchase multiple
paid items as part of a qualifying bundle.
How does the SweetNight Fall Sale discount work?
The Fall Sale uses tiered savings based on the number of paid items
in your order:
CoolNest Ultra alone: 10% off
Buy any 2 paid items: 15% off
Buy any 3 or more paid items: 20% off
The highest qualifying discount is automatically applied at checkout.
Discounts do not stack.
Can I get 10% off when I buy a CoolNest Ultra mattress by itself?
Yes. A CoolNest Ultra Memory Foam Mattress purchased by itself
receives 10% off during the Fall Sale. Other mattresses
do not receive the single-mattress discount. If you add additional
paid items, the 10% Ultra discount is replaced by the applicable
15% or 20% bundle discount.
What counts toward the Buy 2 or Buy 3+ discount?
Paid products count toward the bundle tiers. For example, you can
combine a mattress with a bed frame, sheets, or another qualifying
paid item to reach the 15% or 20% discount tier.
Can I combine the 10%, 15%, and 20% Fall Sale discounts?
No. The Fall Sale discounts do not stack. If your cart qualifies
for multiple tiers, checkout automatically applies only the
highest qualifying discount.
For example, if your order contains three qualifying paid items,
the 20% discount replaces the 15% and 10% discounts.
How does the Build Your Own Mattress Bundle work?
The Build Your Own Mattress Bundle lets you customize your purchase
by selecting a mattress and adding a bed frame, sheets, or other
qualifying items.
A CoolNest Ultra mattress purchased alone receives 10% off. Adding
a second paid item upgrades the order to 15% off, while adding a
third paid item upgrades it to 20% off. Your eligible discount is
calculated automatically at checkout.
Which mattresses are included in the SweetNight Fall Sale?
The Fall Sale features several SweetNight mattresses, including the
CoolNest Ultra Memory Foam Mattress, CoolNest Hybrid Mattress,
CoolNest Memory Foam Mattress, CoolNest Pro Memory Foam Mattress,
and Twilight Hybrid Mattress.
Selected clearance mattresses, including the
Island Hybrid Mattress and Gloaming Hybrid Mattress,
are also featured with additional savings.
Do I need a coupon code for the Fall Sale discount?
No. The Fall Sale discounts are applied automatically at checkout
when your cart meets the requirements for the applicable offer.
No coupon code is required for the 10%, 15%, or 20% automatic
discount tiers.
