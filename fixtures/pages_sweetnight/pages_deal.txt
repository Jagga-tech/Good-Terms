Frequently Asked Questions
What are the main discounts during the Sweetnight Labor Day Sale?
During our Labor Day Sale, you can enjoy up to 45% OFF sitewide on mattresses, bed frames, and sleep accessories. Plus, automatic savings that grow with your cart:
• Buy 1 Mattress, Save 10%
• Buy Any 2 Items, Save 15%
• Buy Any 3 Items, Save 20%
The tiers do not stack — checkout applies the highest one your cart qualifies for. Select mattresses also come with complimentary cooling pillows or free gifts!
Do I need a coupon code to get the Labor Day discounts?
No discount code is required! The sitewide discounts (up to 45% off) and the automatic tiers (10% off 1 mattress / 15% off any 2 items / 20% off any 3 items) will apply at checkout when qualifying items are added to your cart.
Which Sweetnight mattress is best for me during this sale?
It depends on your sleeping position and preferences:
• Hot Sleepers: Try our CoolNest® Series (e.g., CoolNest Ultra) featuring advanced cooling materials and zone support.
• Back Pain & Support Needs: Choose hybrid models like the Twilight Hybrid or Island Hybrid, which combine responsive pocketed coils with pressure-relieving foam.
How does the "Build Your Own Bundle" offer work?
You can mix and match any mattress with bed frames, cooling pillows, or mattress toppers.
• 1 Mattress on its own → 10% OFF
• Any 2 paid items → 15% OFF the total
• Any 3 paid items → 20% OFF the total
Savings are calculated automatically in your shopping cart, and only the highest qualifying tier applies.
Does Sweetnight offer free shipping and a risk-free trial?
Yes! We offer Free Standard Shipping across the contiguous United States. All mattresses bought during the Labor Day Sale are backed by our 100-Night Risk-Free Trial. If you are not satisfied after trying it for at least 30 nights, you can return it for a full refund.
