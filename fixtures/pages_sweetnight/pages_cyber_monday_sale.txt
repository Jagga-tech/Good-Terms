Frequently Asked Questions
When does the SweetNight Cyber Monday Sale start?
+
SweetNight Cyber Monday offers typically launch in late November. You can sign up with your email on our website to receive early bird notifications and exclusive promotional codes before the sale goes live to the public.
What kinds of mattresses are on sale during Cyber Monday?
+
Our Cyber Monday event covers all popular SweetNight collections, including cooling gel memory foam mattresses, targeted multi-zone hybrid mattresses, and motorized adjustable bed frames. Bundles and accessories like pillows and toppers are also included in the holiday discounts.
Do mattresses purchased during Cyber Monday include the 100-night trial?
+
Yes. All mattresses bought during our Cyber Monday and holiday promotional periods come with SweetNight’s standard 100-night home trial. If you are not completely satisfied with your mattress after the break-in period, you can contact our customer support team for an easy return or exchange.
How does Cyber Monday compare to Black Friday for mattress discounts?
+
Both Black Friday and Cyber Monday feature our deepest discounts of the year. If a specific model is already on sale during Black Friday, we recommend securing it early to avoid potential inventory shortages and delivery delays. You can also visit our Black Friday Sale page to compare active promotions.
How can I ensure I get the best price on my SweetNight order?
+
To get the best price, subscribe to our newsletter, check for eligible bundle promotions (such as pairing a mattress with an adjustable base).
