Black Friday Mattress Sale FAQs
When does the SweetNight Black Friday mattress sale start and end?
Early Access is live now and runs through November 15, 2026. The main Black Friday mattress sale runs November 16–29, Cyber Monday deals start November 30, and the event wraps up on December 7, 2026 (Pacific Time). Prices and Flash Deal picks can change between phases, so if something on your list is in stock at a price you like, there's no reason to wait.
What are the best Black Friday mattress deals at SweetNight?
Mattresses are up to 40% off and bundles are up to 50% off. Clearance mattresses, bed frames and bedding go up to 70% off while stock lasts, and Flash Deals take an extra 15%–35% off four of our best sellers. Prices vary by size and thickness, so choose your options on each product page to see the price you'll actually pay.
How does Buy More, Save More work?
Put any 2 eligible items in your cart and you get an extra 15% off. Make it 3 or more and the extra discount goes up to 20%. The savings are calculated automatically at checkout. Flash Deal prices can't be combined with the extra discount; when more than one offer applies, checkout gives you the best eligible price.
Do I need a promo code for SweetNight Black Friday deals?
No. Sale prices, Buy More, Save More savings and Flash Deal prices all apply automatically. Add what you want to your cart and the discount shows up at checkout.
What is a Black Friday Flash Deal?
Flash Deals are four limited-quantity offers on our most popular products, each with an extra 15%–35% off. New picks drop every 3 days. Every deal has its own countdown and a set number of units, so once it sells out or the timer runs out, that price is gone.
Which mattress should I buy during the Black Friday sale?
Start with how you sleep:
• You sleep hot and want our best: the CoolNest® Ultra 7-zone cooling memory foam mattress is our flagship, pairing multi-stage cooling with zoned support.
• You like the bounce of coils: the CoolNest® Hybrid Mattress combines responsive coils with CoolNest® cooling layers and a pillow top.
• You want a proven best seller for less: the CoolNest® Memory Foam Mattress is our most popular cooling model.
Still comparing? See all of our picks for the best cooling mattress side by side.
Is Black Friday a good time to buy a mattress?
Usually, yes. Black Friday is when most mattress brands, SweetNight included, run their deepest discounts of the year, and bundles are where the savings add up fastest. If you're weighing it against other holiday sales, our guide to Labor Day vs. Black Friday mattress deals breaks down how they compare.
Do Black Friday mattress orders still get free shipping and the 100-night trial?
Yes. Buying on sale doesn't change your coverage. Mattresses ship free by standard shipping within the contiguous U.S., and every mattress comes with our 100-night sleep trial, so you can sleep on it at home before deciding to keep it.
Can I use HSA/FSA funds or pay over time?
Yes. Many of our mattresses are HSA/FSA eligible; look for the HSA/FSA label on the product card. You can also choose Affirm at checkout to split the cost into payments, with 0% APR options for eligible buyers.
