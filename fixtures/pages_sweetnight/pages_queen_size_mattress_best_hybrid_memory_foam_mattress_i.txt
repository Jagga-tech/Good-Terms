What size is a queen mattress?
The queen size mattress is 60 inches wide and 80 inches long and perhaps the most versatile for all sleepers. This size mattresses give sleepers extra space to sleep comfortably and move around in bed, while still fitting in small bedrooms. It is traditional used in master room.
What's a good price for a mattress?
Generally, most mattresses cost between $250 to even $2,000+, and some premium mattresses can cost $5,000 or more. Many factors contribute to the price: the mattress size, materials used, layering, different brands and some added features like waterproofing and more.
