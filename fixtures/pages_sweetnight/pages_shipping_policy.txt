Special Order Statement For U.S.
Remote region orders will be charged an extra delivery fee by FedEx or UPS, depending upon the distance to the destination.
Alaska, Hawaii, Puerto Rico, Guam, the U.S. Virgin Islands or to Military APO/FPO addresses are not in our delivery zone at the moment,but we will have more steps to support these areas soon
Additional Charge Situation
We encourage you to check the address details carefully before you summit the order,if you need to change the address during the delivering or delivered period,the additional service fees would be charged maximum up to $40 per shipment which depend on the specific situations,as well as the return requested by mistaken order.

Frequently Asked Questions
What shipping methods does SweetNight offer?
Most SweetNight products ship for free via standard FedEx delivery within the U.S. in approximately 3–5 business days after processing.
How long does delivery take once I place my order?
Orders are typically processed quickly and delivered in about 2–6 business days to most U.S. addresses, depending on the delivery location.
When will my order be shipped?
Orders placed on weekdays are generally shipped the next business day, while weekend orders ship the following Monday.
Are mattress deliveries available outside the contiguous U.S.?
SweetNight currently focuses on delivery within the continental U.S.; remote locations may incur additional fees, and some regions like Alaska, Hawaii, and military addresses may not be in the delivery zone yet.
How can I track my SweetNight delivery?
Once your order is shipped, you’ll receive tracking information so you can monitor delivery progress to your address. (Shipping confirmations are typically emailed after the order is processed.)
