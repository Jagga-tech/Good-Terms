Frequently Asked Questions
Are full memory foam mattresses good?
There’s a lot to like about a full size memory foam mattress. Firstly, memory foam mattress is often constructed with materials that are hypoallergenic and dust-mite resistant. The multiple layer structure contributes to the ultimate comfort. It gently conforms to your body and reduces pressure on your spine for your best rest possible.
As for the full memory foam mattress, it provides enough space for single sleepers who are prone to stretch out as they sleep and those like to sleep with their pets. The full is also an ideal choice if you live in a studio apartment and needs more room to fit other furniture.
What thickness memory foam is best?
Most high-quality mattresses are between 8 to 14 inches thick, but the thickness doesn’t necessarily equate to comfort. It’s subjective and more about your individual preferences, body weight and sleeping style,etc. For example, side sleepers may find a thicker mattress provides more cushioning around pressure points than thinner ones.

Which mattress is best for back pain?
The best mattresses for back pain offer ample support to keep your spine properly aligned as you snooze, but also take the pressure off your joints and let your body fully relax. While there are personal preferences to consider, a medium-firm mattress is typically the best option for people with back pain.
