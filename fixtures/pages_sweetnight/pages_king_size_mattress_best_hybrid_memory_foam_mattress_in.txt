Which is the best king size mattress?

Which mattress is best for back pain?
The best mattresses for back pain offer ample support to keep your spine properly aligned as you snooze, but also take the pressure off your joints and let your body fully relax. While there are personal preferences to consider, a medium-firm mattress is typically the best option for people with back pain.

How many person can sleep in king size bed?
In most cases, a king mattress is best for couples to sleep comfortably. However, if a couple has a baby, they can comfortably include the toddler and still fit on the king size while leaving enough space between them.
Being 76 inches at width, yes, mathematically, it could fit more than three adults, but it is not a good idea to have more than 2 adults share a king bed, cause you might find the bed quite uncomfortable.
Will I sleep better in a king size bed?
A king size mattress is also versatile for all types of sleepers, with its spacious dimensions. It can best fit couples like to enjoy individual space. Single adults who are combination sleepers may love the ample space a king bed allows, giving them plenty of room to change positions comfortably. For a couple with babies, a king mattress is the ideal size to accommodate the entire family.
