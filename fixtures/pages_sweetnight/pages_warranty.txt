Mattress — 10-Year Limited Warranty
What's Covered
Under normal household use, this warranty covers:
Visible indentation greater than 1 inch (≈2.5 cm) that remains after the mattress has fully recovered from load. This indentation must not be caused by an inadequate or unsupportive foundation or bed frame.
Cracks or splits in the foam that occur despite normal usage and proper handling.
Manufacturing defects in the cover — including seams that come apart, zippers that fail due to faulty assembly, or stitching that unravels.
Failure to expand after unpacking — if the mattress has not fully expanded to its intended dimensions after 72 hours of being left unpacked in a well-ventilated room at room temperature, and the deviation clearly exceeds normal dimensional tolerance.
Other verified issues that our technical assessment team mutually agrees are directly attributable to inherent defects in materials or workmanship.
What's Not Covered
Normal softening of the foam over time, which is expected during the break-in period of any memory foam or visco-elastic product and does not compromise its pressure-relieving properties.
Damage caused by abuse, misuse, neglect, improper handling, or failure to follow care instructions.
Comfort preferences — if you decide the mattress is too soft, too firm, or not to your personal liking. (That's what our [100-Night Mattress Sleep Trial] is for!)
Products sold "as-is," floor models, samples, or products purchased second-hand.

Standard Bed Frame — 2-Year Limited Warranty
What's Covered
Structural defects in the frame, including cracks, breaks, or warping of the wood or metal frame components
Manufacturing defects in joints, connectors, or hardware
Defects in the finish or coating that occur despite normal use (e.g., peeling, flaking)
Other verified issues that our technical assessment team mutually agrees are directly attributable to inherent defects in materials or workmanship.
What's Not Covered
Damage caused by abuse, misuse, neglect, improper assembly, or failure to follow assembly instructions
Normal wear and tear, including scratches, dents, or fading from regular use
Damage caused by exceeding the weight capacity of the frame
Comfort preferences or aesthetic preferences
Stains, tears, burns, or liquid damage caused by the user
Mold, mildew, or odors resulting from improper care or environmental conditions
Products repaired or modified by unauthorized service providers
Claims where the Warranty Card and/or Order Information cannot be provided, or have been altered
SweetNight standard (non-electric) bed frames are indoor furniture products covered by a 2-Year Limited Warranty against defects in materials and workmanship under normal household use.
Electronic Accessories: Certain standard bed frame models may include electronic components such as LED light strips or built-in USB outlets. These electronic accessories, where present, are covered by a 1-Year Limited Warranty. The 2-Year Limited Warranty continues to apply to all structural frame components.

Control boxes, remote controls, USB ports, power supplies, and all power cords/cables.

Pillow — 1-Year Limited Warranty
What's Covered
This Limited Pillow Warranty covers issues caused by defects in workmanship or materials, subject to the limitations set forth in this warranty:
Clumping, shifting, or uneven distribution of filling materials that creates noticeable lumps or flat spots and cannot be restored by fluffing.
Seams that come apart or stitching that unravels due to manufacturing defects.
Defects in the pillow cover (holes, tears, or stains) that are present upon arrival. If the pillow is still unopened, this may be handled through our Return Policy; if already opened, it is covered under this warranty.
Other verified issues that our technical assessment team mutually agrees are directly attributable to inherent defects in materials or workmanship.
What's Not Covered
Normal softening or gradual loss of loft over time that is consistent with regular use of foam and fiber pillows.
Comfort preferences — if the pillow's height, firmness, or feel is not to your personal liking.
Damage caused by improper washing, drying, or failure to follow instructions on the care label.
Bedding — 1-Year Limited Warranty
What's Covered
This Limited Bedding Warranty covers issues caused by defects in workmanship or materials, subject to the limitations set forth in this warranty:
Seams that come apart or stitching that unravels due to manufacturing defects.
Defects in the fabric (holes, tears, or stains) that are present upon arrival. If the bedding is still unopened, this may be handled through our Return Policy; if already opened, it is covered under this warranty.
Hardware defects (snaps, buttons, or zippers) that fail due to faulty assembly or defective materials.
Other verified issues that our technical assessment team mutually agrees are directly attributable to inherent defects in materials or workmanship.
What's Not Covered
Normal softening, pilling, or gradual fading over time that is consistent with regular use and washing of textile products.
Comfort preferences — if the sheets' texture, softness, or feel is not to your personal liking.
Damage caused by improper washing, drying, bleaching, or failure to follow instructions on the care label.
Stains, tears, burns, or liquid damage caused by the user after delivery.
General Terms & Conditions
Original Purchaser Only. All warranties apply exclusively to the original purchaser and are non-transferable. Proof of purchase (Order ID, Warranty Card) is required.
No Renewal. A warranty replacement, repair, or parts replacement does not extend or renew the warranty period. The warranty continues from the original purchase date.
Valid Purchase Channels. Warranties are valid for products purchased from sweetnight.com or authorized distributors/dealers. If you purchased from a third-party retailer, please check that retailer's warranty policies.
Subjective Preferences. This warranty DOES NOT cover subjective preferences regarding comfort, firmness, height, or appearance. If your product includes a trial period, comfort-related concerns should be addressed through that trial within the applicable window. Once any applicable trial or return period has expired, claims based on personal preference — including a product feeling too soft, too firm, too high, or too low — will not be accepted under this warranty.
User Damage & Care Neglect. This warranty strictly excludes damage caused by user abuse, pets, or neglectful care. This includes stains, rips, tears, cuts, burns, or liquid damage. Additionally, mold, mildew, or persistent odors resulting from improper laundering, spills, or failure to maintain adequate ventilation are not covered.
Modifications. SweetNight reserves the right to modify these terms. Changes will be effective upon posting to this page. Existing warranty claims will be handled under the terms in effect at the time of purchase.
