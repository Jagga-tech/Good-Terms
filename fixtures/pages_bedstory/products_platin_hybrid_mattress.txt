Frequently Asked Questions
Is the Platin mattress extra firm?
Yes. The BedStory Platin Hybrid Mattress is designed with an extra-firm feel and minimal sinkage, creating a stable, grounded sleep surface for sleepers who prefer stronger support.
Who is the Platin Extra Firm Hybrid Mattress best for?
Platin is especially suited for back sleepers, stomach sleepers, and anyone who prefers a firmer mattress with less sinkage and more stable full-body support.
Is an extra firm mattress good for back sleepers?
Back sleepers who prefer a firmer feel may benefit from the stable, low-sinkage support of an extra firm mattress. Platin is designed to provide consistent support across the body without excessive sinking.
Is the Platin mattress good for stomach sleepers?
Yes. Its extra-firm feel helps limit excessive sinkage around the hips and midsection, making it a suitable choice for stomach sleepers who prefer a more supportive sleep surface.
Does the Platin mattress reduce motion transfer?
Yes. Individually wrapped pocket coils move independently, helping reduce motion transfer when one sleeper changes position during the night.
What is the Platin Hybrid Mattress made of?
Platin combines supportive foam layers with individually wrapped pocket coils to create an extra-firm hybrid mattress with responsive support, minimal sinkage, and improved motion isolation.
Is the Platin mattress fiberglass-free?
Yes. The Platin mattress is designed with a fiberglass-free construction and uses CertiPUR-US® certified foam and OEKO-TEX® certified materials.
Is the Platin mattress a mattress in a box?
Yes. The Platin Hybrid Mattress is compressed and conveniently shipped in a box. After unboxing, allow the mattress time to fully expand before use.
How long does the Platin mattress take to expand?
After unboxing, the mattress will begin expanding immediately. For the best shape and comfort, allow 48-72 hours for it to fully recover before sleeping on it.
