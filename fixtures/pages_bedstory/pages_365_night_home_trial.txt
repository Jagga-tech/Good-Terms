I.A. Applicable Products

I.B.When trial period start?
The 365-night trial period begins on the date of delivery.
I.C.Does the trial policy apply to third-party retailers?
1. Order from BedStory official → 365-night trial applies.
2. Order from Amazon → Contact BedStory Amazon Customer Service.
3. Other Retail: Contact the platform customer service.
BedStory is not responsible for the services of other third-party retailers.
I.D.Are sale or discounted items eligible for trial policy?
On BedStory's official website, regular-priced items are eligible for a refund, but sale items may not be. Verify sale item return eligibility at the time of purchase.
II. Return & Refund
II.A.What if I'm not satisfied during the trial?
If you decide the mattress isn't right for you at any time during the 365-night period, contact BedStory's customer service team to start the return process. They will guide you through the steps and arrange pickup.
More return info: https://bedstory.com/pages/return-policy
II.B.How long does the return process take?
Once the return request is approved, refund typically takes 5-7 business days back to your original payment method account
II.C.Do I need to keep the original packaging for a return?
For a full return, it's recommended to keep the original packaging if possible. If your return is missing original packaging, request may be rejected.
II.D.Who pays for return shipping if I change my mind?
Free return for collapse or damage. However, if the return is for personal reasons, you will need to cover the shipping costs.
