Warranty Policy
Everything you need to know about
I. What does the BedStory warranty cover?
Visible sagging greater than 2 inches.
Foam cracking or splitting under normal use.
Coil or structural failure.
Zipper or cover defects (if applicable).
II. What is NOT covered under warranty?
Normal softening or comfort preference changes.
Improper use (e.g., unsupported foundation).
Stains, spills, or physical damage.
Minor body impressions (< 2 inches).
If you're UNCERTAIN whether your issue qualifies for coverage, our team will help you determine eligibility before you submit a claim.
III. How long does coverage last?
BedStory mattresses are built for long-term durability and backed by an extended limited warranty.
Designed to support you for years of restful sleep.
IV. Is the warranty transferable?
The warranty is valid only for the original purchaser and is non-transferable.
V. Do I need a specific bed frame for warranty coverage?
Yes — proper support is essential:
Slatted base with slats ≤ 3 inches apart.
Box spring or adjustable base.
Solid, flat foundation.
Using the right foundation helps prevent sagging and ensures your warranty remains valid.
VI. How is this different from the 365-night trial?
365-Night Trial → Try the comfort, risk-free.
Warranty → Protects against defects long-term.
VII. How do I file a warranty claim?
Contact our support team.
Provide photos of the issue.
Share your order details.
Our team will review and respond within 48 hours.
VIII. Will I need to return the mattress?
Not always.
In most cases, we evaluate based on photos.
If needed, we’ll guide you through the next steps.
No complicated shipping required for verified defects.
We make the process as easy as possible for you.
IX. What if I feel the mattress is too firm or too soft?
Comfort is personal — and that's exactly why we offer a 365-night trial.
Give your body at least 30 nights to adjust.
If it’s still not the right fit, we’ll help with a return or exchange.
Most comfort-related issues are resolved during the trial period — not through warranty claims.
X. Why can I trust BedStory?
Trusted by thousands of families across the U.S.
Premium materials (CertiPUR-US® certified foams).
Engineered for multi-generational sleep support.
Backed by our Warm Guardian philosophy.
Because every bed we build is meant to protect a story — including yours.
