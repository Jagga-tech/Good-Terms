Is a cooling mattress good for hot sleepers?
+-
Cooling Collection is designed with breathable layers and cooling gel to keep you comfortable all night.
Does cooling technology really work?
+-
Yes, they do. Materials like PCMs, gel-infused foam, breathable covers, and POE Air Fiber layers have been shown to help regulate your mattress's temperature.
What's the main difference between an active and a passive cooling mattress?
+-
An active cooling mattress uses powered technology, such as fans, water circulation, or thermoelectric systems, to remove heat.
A passive cooling mattress uses breathable materials such as latex, cotton, or wool, as well as moisture-wicking fabrics, to naturally regulate temperature without electricity.
Are cooling mattresses good for back pain?
+-
Yes. A comfortable cooling mattress, such as a hybrid, can help keep you at a good temperature and let your muscles relax. It also supports your spine, which can make sleep better and may help with back pain.
What's the difference between BEDSTORY SPINALIGN™ and COOLING COLLECTION?
+-
BEDSTORY SPINALIGN™ is designed for firm, spinal support. COOLING COLLECTION focuses on cooling comfort with breathable layers and an ice-knit surface—perfect for hot sleepers.
Does the SPINEALIGN have good motion isolation and edge support?
+-
Yes. The individual pocketed springs minimize motion transfer, so you won't be disturbed by a partner moving during the night. The reinforced edges also provide sturdy support, preventing sagging when sitting or sleeping near the side of the bed.
Does it have cooling features?
+-
Unlike the COOLING COLLECTION, the SPINEALIGN mattress doesn't have dedicated cooling technologies, such as phase-change materials. It focuses primarily on spinal alignment and firm support, though the breathable knit cover and open-coil system do disperse heat.
Is BEDSTORY SPINEALIGN good for back pain?
+-
Definitely. It comes with a hybrid designed for their zoned coils and balanced support. They combine foam that shapes to your body with coils that respond to movement, helping keep your spine aligned and easing pressure for many sleepers.
Who is SPINEALIGN best for?
+-
The SPINEALIGN is Firm to Medium-Firm.
Recommended for:
1. Back sleepers: natural spinal curve.
2. Stomach sleepers: prevent the hips from sinking too deeply.
3. People with chronic back pain.
4. Those who need extra edge support: reinforced edges prevent mattress sagging.
Which sleeping positions are NOT recommended for the SPINEALIGN?
+-
Firm support of BEDSTORY SPINEALIGN makes it less ideal for side sleepers who need softer pressure relief for hips and shoulders. Side sleepers may prefer the COOLING COLLECTION mattress.
What is the good firm mattress​?
+-
Four key features for a good firm mattress:
1. Reinforced pocket coils for minimal top cushioning.
2. Strong edge support for full surface use, and no noticeable sinkage under hips or shoulders.
3. A responsive surface that prevents spinal curvature for back and stomach sleepers.
4. High-density foam that resists sagging, and a breathable cover to offset the rigid feel.
Is a firm mattress good for your back?
+-
Medium-firm mattresses are generally better for chronic back pain. Responsive support surface prevents sagging while still offering enough contouring to relieve pressure.
What is a medium-firm mattress?
+-
A medium-firm mattress provides balanced support and contouring to relieve pressure at the hips and shoulders, which is good for side sleepers, yet firm enough to prevent spinal misalignment for back and stomach sleepers.
What is the difference between a plush and a firm mattress​?
+-
A plush mattress has a soft top layer that relieves pressure and a firm core that lets side sleepers sink in a bit. In contrast, a firm mattress feels hard and barely sinks, making it better for stomach and back sleepers who need strong spinal support.
Is an extra-firm mattress good for back pain?
+-
Yes, the BedStory Extra firm mattress provides a firmer lower back zone for the lumbar curve to relieve back pain, and soft shoulder and hip zones for pressure relief.
Is an extra firm mattress good for side sleepers?
+-
An extra-firm mattress is generally NOT suitable for side sleepers and is often considered the worst choice for this position. This type of mattress can lead to two significant issues:
1. Pressure Point Pain: An extra-firm mattress exerts substantial pressure on your body, particularly on your shoulder joint and the prominent area of your hip.
2. Spinal Misalignment: To alleviate this pressure, your body may naturally shift positions. You might end up sleeping with your arm tucked under your body or with your hips rolled forward, which can cause your spine to twist and move out of its neutral, straight alignment.
What is the difference between a firm and an extra-firm mattress?
+-
The key difference between a firm mattress and an extra-firm mattress is the level of cushioning. While both offer strong support, an extra-firm mattress has little to no cushioning, creating a rigid, "on top of the bed" feel.
Who is an extra firm mattress best for?
+-
Extra firm mattresses are ideal for:
1. Stomach sleepers: An extra-firm surface prevents hips from sinking, helping maintain spinal alignment.
2. Back sleepers: Lumbar support keeps the spine neutral.
3. Heavier individuals (230+ lbs): Extra firm support prevents the body from sinking.
Who should NOT buy an extra firm mattress?
+-
1. A side sleeper – An extra-firm sleep surface creates painful pressure points at the shoulders and hips.
2. A lightweight sleeper (under 130 lbs) – you won't weigh enough to get any contouring.
3. Someone who prefers a plush, sinking-in feel.
For side sleepers, BedStory's Plush COOLING COLLECTION Series is a better option, as it offers pressure relief for the hips and shoulders.
What is a plush mattress?
+-
A plush mattress has a soft, cushioned top layer and firm support underneath. It works well for side sleepers and lighter people because it eases pressure on the hips and shoulders while keeping the spine aligned.
What is the difference between a plush and a firm mattress​?
+-
A plush mattress has a soft top layer that relieves pressure and a firm core that lets side sleepers sink in a bit. In contrast, a firm mattress feels hard and barely sinks, making it better for stomach and back sleepers who need strong spinal support.
Is a plush mattress good for back pain?
+-
Plush mattresses are usually best for side sleepers because they cushion your hips and shoulders and help keep your spine straight. But if you sleep on your back or stomach, a plush mattress might hurt your alignment and cause back pain. Most people with back pain are advised to use medium-to-firm mattresses.
Pillow top plush mattress vs Euro top plush mattress
+-
A pillow-top plush mattress has a soft, thick pad sewn on top, giving it a cloud-like feel and letting you sink in more. A Euro-top plush mattress has similar padding, but it is sewn to the mattress edges, which makes it look neater and provides firmer edge support.
Do BedStory mattress toppers help with back pain?
+-
Absolutely. The ergonomic design conforms to the body's curves, evenly distributes weight, and reduces tension in the shoulders, hips, and back—making them especially beneficial for those with chronic pain or poor posture.
Does the topper help with cooling?
+-
The topper uses high-density memory foam infused with bamboo charcoal to absorb moisture, eliminate odors, and help keep the bed fresh throughout the night. However, for a hot sleeper, a cooling collection mattress is the most recommended.
Can I use the topper as an RV mattress?
+-
Yes. Portable design makes the mattress topper easy to carry, for camping, RV mattresses, or dormitory use.
Do the mattress topper be washed?
+-
A premium zippered cover for quick removal, machine washable to keep it clean. The inner foam core should never be washed or submerged in water.
Does it slide around on the bed?
+-
Toppers all come with four corner elastic straps to secure the topper to the mattress.

What is the difference between a full and a queen-size mattress​?
+-
The main difference between a Full and a Queen mattress is their size.
A Full mattress has the following specifications:
👉Dimensions:** 54" x 74"
👉Minimum room size:** 9'6" x 10'6" to 10' x 10'
👉Maximum sleep height:** Less than 6'
👉Best for:** Teens and single adults
A Queen mattress, on the other hand, has these specifications:
👉Dimensions:** 60" x 80"
👉Minimum room size:** 10' x 10' to 10' x 12'
👉Maximum sleep height:** 6' to 6'4"
👉Best for:** Couples
For a single adult, a Full mattress is a great space-saver, while a Queen provides more luxury and future-proofing. However, for couples, a Queen mattress is typically considered the minimum size. Sharing a Full mattress may lead to disturbed sleep, especially if one or both partners tend to move around frequently, sleep on their stomachs or backs, or are larger in build.
Can a queen mattress fit in a minivan?
+-
You can fit a standard queen mattress (60" W x 80" L) in most minivans with proper angling.
Here’s how popular models handle it:
1. Toyota Sienna: Fits well with a slight angle.
2. Honda Odyssey: Fits well; removing the third-row seats maximizes space.
3. Chrysler Pacifica: Fits flat, but may need slight bending for width.
4. Dodge Caravan: Fits with angling due to wheel well intrusions.
Who is a Twin mattress best for?
+-
1. Children and teenagers – Perfect size for growing kids.
2. College students – Fits standard dorm room beds.
3. Guest rooms – Space-efficient for occasional guests
4. Small bedrooms – Maximizes floor space in compact rooms.
What is a split king mattress?
+-
A split king mattress is made up of two separate Twin XL mattresses, each measuring 38 inches by 80 inches, positioned side by side. This arrangement provides the same overall dimensions as a standard king-size bed. It is specifically designed for couples who have different comfort preferences, allowing them to enjoy two independent sleep spaces within one shared bed.
