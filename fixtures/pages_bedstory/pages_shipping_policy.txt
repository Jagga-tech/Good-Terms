Shipping Policy
Rest Easy-We've Got Delivery Covered
Free Shipping on All Orders
We offer complimentary shipping within the continental U.S. No hidden fees, ever.
Carriers & Delivery Time
Your order will be shipped via FedEx. Processing Time: 1–2 business days. Estimated Delivery Time: 2–5 business days (depending on your location)
Where We Don’t Ship
Currently, we’re unable to deliver to Hawaii (HI), Virgin Islands (VI), Alaska (AK), Puerto Rico (PR), or to addresses with DPO, FPO, and APO. We're working to expand our coverage in the future.
Can I specify a delivery date?
Yes, you can request a preferred delivery date. Please email our support team after placing your order to confirm availability.
Follow Your Order
About 24 hours after your order ships, you’ll receive a confirmation email with a tracking number. Simply click the link or enter the number on the carrier’s website (FedEx) to check your delivery status anytime.
