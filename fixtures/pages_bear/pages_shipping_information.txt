How To Unpack Your Mattress
We recommend you unboxing your mattress within 30 days.
We recommend having a friend or two help set up your new mattress.
Remove the mattress from the box in the bedroom you would like to keep it in.
Place vacuum-sealed mattress onto your bed base.
Unroll the first layer of plastic
Unfold the mattress and align the top of the mattress with the top of the bed frame.
Carefully use scissors to cut away the plastic wrapping. Your mattress will begin to expand immediately!
Cut the plastic around the perimeter of the mattress and remove.
The bed should be completely expanded in less than a minute!
While your mattress can be used right away, we recommend giving it a full 24hrs to completely decompress.
Frequently Asked Questions
Can I specify a delivery date?
If you would like to change anything regarding the delivery of your Bear products we recommend signing up for Fedex Delivery Manager. This free membership allows you to customize delivery times, request vacation holds, and even request the package to be held at a local Fedex facility for future pick up or deliver to a different address.
How long can my mattress stay in the box?
We recommend you unbox you mattress within 30 days of delivery.
How much is shipping?
Shipping is free to the US lower 48 states. Additional fees may apply for shipping to Alaska or Hawaii.
Do I need to be home for the delivery?
No. FedEx will drop off your Bear products right to your home (where you normally receive large packages). No signature is required, and you do not need to be home to accept delivery. If you need to edit your delivery options, please visit Fedex Delivery Manager for more details.
Can I request Signature Required?
Yes. We now offer the option for Signature Required (Mattresses ONLY) at no cost to you!
