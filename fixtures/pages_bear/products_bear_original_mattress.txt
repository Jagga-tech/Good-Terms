Restorative Rest with Sleep Recovery Technology™
Sleep Recovery Technology™ is built into the Bear experience. Our mattress covers feature advanced fibers that capture body heat, convert it to infrared energy, and reflect it back. By boosting cellular oxygenation, Celliant® helps you:
Regulate temperature
Recover quicker from physical activity
Fall asleep faster and stay asleep longer*
*Source: CELLIANT Pilot Sleep Study Dr. Marcel Hungs & Dr. Annabel Wang, University of California - Irvine Medical Center, 2010. Results not achieved by all subjects in the study. Further studies are warranted before drawing conclusions as to CELLIANT® sleep benefits.
Cushioning Comfort for Aches and Pains
Plush foams cradle your body after long, demanding days. By redistributing your weight, this gentle comfort system eases tension around sensitive joints and pressure points. With your muscles relaxed, you can drift off into restorative sleep.
A Firm Foundation for Support That Lasts
This all-foam mattress is built on a high-density foam base that promotes proper spinal alignment, resists sagging over time, and reinforces the comfort layers above for a consistently stable and structured feel.
Your Sleep Recovery Stack
Every layer of the Bear Original is carefully crafted to promote rejuvenating rest, night after night.
1.
Quilted Celliant® Infrared Cover
What It Is
Plush and protective cover fabric is infused with fibers that convert excess body heat into infrared energy.
How It Helps
Infrared energy increases local circulation and cellular oxygenation, giving strained muscles an overnight recovery boost.
2.
Gel Swirl Memory Foam
What It Is
Soothing memory foam infused with gel for its cooling effect.
How It Helps
This gentle foam hugs your curves without trapping warmth. Instead, the gel actively draws heat away from your body and disperses it for quick relief.
3.
Responsive Comfort Foam
What It Is
An extra comfort layer designed to deepen pressure relief and enhance ease of movement.
How It Helps
Firmer foam with a slight bounce lifts and reinforces the layers above for complete comfort without that “sinking” feel.
4.
High-Density Foundation Foam
What It Is
Six inches of resilient, high-density base foam.
How It Helps
The foam support core gives the mattress its structure and strength, delivering deep support that stands the test of time.

What’s the difference between the Bear Original and the Bear Original Hybrid?
The difference comes down to the support system.
While the Bear Original is an all-foam mattress with a close-contouring feel, the Origial Hybrid features foam comfort layers and a coil support core. The coils provide:
Deeper support and durability for long-term performance
A more responsive feel for easier movement
Better airflow for cooler sleep
What makes the Bear Original a best value mattress?
The Bear Original is considered a best value mattress because it delivers reliable comfort and support at an accessible price.
Its all-foam design combines soothing foam comfort layers (including cool gel-infused memory foam) with a high-density base for durability. This relieves pressure and keeps the spine aligned, covering all the essentials at a great value.
