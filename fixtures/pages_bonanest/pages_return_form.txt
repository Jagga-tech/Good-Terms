At Bonanest
We strive to redefine the furniture discovery experience and make a curated home more attainable.
You might have seen us on TikTok
With more than 19,000 followers, 46,000 likes, and a growing community of furniture lovers, BonaNest started by helping people discover furniture in a more fun, visual, and approachable way. Today, shoppers can explore more than 200 pieces across living rooms, bedrooms, dining spaces, home offices, kids’ rooms, and beyond—whether they are searching for an everyday essential or the statement piece that brings the whole room together.
BonaNest began on TikTok with a simple idea: furniture shopping should feel more inspiring, approachable, and connected to the way people discover new styles today. By sharing furniture through engaging content and real-life inspiration, we set out to make finding the right pieces feel less overwhelming and more personal.
Our Roots
BonaNest was founded by a team with more than two decades of experience across furniture design, manufacturing, and retail. We combined that industry knowledge with the energy and accessibility of social media to create a more modern way to discover furniture.
Based in Southern California, BonaNest offers a wide range of designs created for different homes, lifestyles, and personal tastes. Our collections include furniture for living rooms, dining spaces, bedrooms, entertainment areas, children’s rooms, smaller homes, and more elevated interiors.
