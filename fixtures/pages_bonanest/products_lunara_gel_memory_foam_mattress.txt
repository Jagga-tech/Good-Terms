Description
Description
Upgrade your sleep without compromising your health or your budget. Engineered for deep support and peace of mind, this premium memory foam mattress delivers exceptional pressure relief while remaining 100% fiberglass-free. Designed with a breathable cover and fast-response foam layers, it regulates temperature throughout the night so you sleep cool and wake up refreshed.
Shipped conveniently in a box directly to your door, we offer a massive range of profiles and sizes so you can find the exact fit for any frame, room, or sleeper.
How to Choose Your Perfect Thickness (Select from Dropdown):
5" & 6" Profiles: The ultimate space-savers. Perfect for bunk beds, trundles, RVs, camper vans, and dorm rooms where clearance is limited.
8" & 10" Profiles: The everyday standard. Excellent choices for growing kids, teenagers, guest rooms, and budget-conscious adult sleepers.
12" & 14" Profiles: Maximum support and durability. Built with massive, heavy-duty base layers ideal for master bedrooms and primary adult use.

Key Features & Sleep Benefits:
100% Fiberglass-Free: Sleep safely. Our fire-retardant barrier is made without harmful woven fiberglass, ensuring your home and indoor air quality remain completely protected and toxin-free.
CertiPUR-US® Certified: The interior foams are rigorously tested to be free of ozone depleters, heavy metals, formaldehyde, and phthalates, with low VOC emissions for clean indoor air.
Breathable, Fast-Response Memory Foam: The top comfort layer features an open-cell (30D) memory foam design. Unlike dense, traditional memory foams that trap heat and make you feel "stuck," this lightweight layer breathes easily and adapts instantly to your body.
Medium Firm, Heavy-Duty Core Support: Built on a foundation of high-density (25D) base foam, this mattress prevents deep sagging, aligns your spine, and provides excellent edge-to-edge structural stability. Ideal for back and stomach sleepers who need substantial support.
Cooling Knit Cover: Wrapped in a durable, 100% polyester knit cover that wicks away moisture and promotes airflow directly at the sleep surface.
Easy Bed-in-a-Box Setup: Compressed, vacuum-sealed, and neatly rolled into a single box. Simply unbox, unroll, and watch it safely expand to its full profile within 24 to 48 hours.
Mattress Construction Details:
Top Layer: Breathable, contouring memory foam for immediate pressure relief.
Base Layer: High-density standard foam block for deep spinal alignment.
Comfort Level: Firm (Best for back/stomach sleepers or those who prefer solid support).
Safety Standard: Meets 16 CFR 1633 Flammability Standards (No Fiberglass).
