Refund policy
Return Policy
We have a 30-day return policy, which means you have 30 days after receiving your item to request a return.
To be eligible for a return, your item must be in the same condition that you received it, unworn or unused, with tags, and in its original packaging. You’ll also need the receipt or proof of purchase.
To start a return, you can contact us at bonanestcs@gmail.com.
If your return is accepted, we’ll send you instructions on how and where to send your package. Items sent back to us without first requesting a return will not be accepted.
For any questions regarding returns, please contact us at bonanestcs@gmail.com.
Mattress Return Conditions
For mattress returns, the mattress must be unrolled but still in its original compressed and deflated condition in order to be eligible for a return.
If the mattress has been fully expanded or inflated, the return may still be accepted, however the customer will be responsible for the return shipping cost.
Large Furniture Returns
For larger furniture items such as bed frames or bedroom sets, the customer is responsible for the return shipping cost due to the size and freight requirements of these items.
Damages and Issues
Please inspect your order upon delivery and contact us immediately if the item is defective, damaged, or if you receive the wrong item so that we can evaluate the issue and make it right.
For assistance, please contact bonanestcs@gmail.com.
Exceptions / Non-Returnable Items
Certain types of items cannot be returned, including:
• Perishable goods (such as food, flowers, or plants)
• Custom products (such as special orders or personalized items)
• Personal care goods (such as beauty products)
• Hazardous materials, flammable liquids, or gases
Please contact us at bonanestcs@gmail.com if you have questions about whether your item is eligible for return.
Unfortunately, sale items and gift cards cannot be returned.
Exchanges
The fastest way to ensure you receive the item you want is to return the item you currently have, and once the return is accepted, place a separate order for the new item.
Refunds
We will notify you once we have received and inspected your return, and inform you whether your refund has been approved.
If approved, your refund will be automatically issued to your original payment method within 10 business days. Please note that your bank or credit card company may require additional time to process and post the refund.
If more than 15 business days have passed since your return was approved, please contact us at bonanestcs@gmail.com.
