Details
✅This 10-inch midsize luxury hybrid mattress features edge support to help you sleep peacefully. The mattress is made of independent springs, corrugated cotton, and anti-static fabric. It has moderate hardness and low motion transmission rate, so your partner's movements will not disturb your rest. Additionally, the mattress is breathable and hypoallergenic, making it ideal for sensitive sleepers. The mattress is designed to sit on a box spring or slats and is compatible with adjustable beds. This mattress ships compressed in the box, so please allow up to 48-72 hours for the mattress to fully inflate before use.
Package Included:
✅1 xMattress
Mattress size:
✅10"Queen Size：80.00"L x 60.00"W x 10.00"H
【Isolated Motion & Reduced Interference】Our hybrid mattress features hundreds of individually wrapped springs. This innovative design ensures not only that there is no friction between the springs, but also that motion transfer is minimized, allowing you and your partner to enjoy undisturbed, restful sleep.
【Certified Safe Materials】Crafted from high-quality CertiPUR-US certified foam and wrapped with an OEKO-TEX certified mattress cover, these materials ensure the use of health and skin-friendly materials. Additionally, it features SGS-certified fireproof cotton, ensuring maximum safety and a healthier sleep environment.
【Strong Support & Longevity】Our hybrid mattress features larger springs, with a cross-sectional area 23.5% larger than traditional springs, crafted from high-strength carbon alloy steel. These upgraded springs provide enhanced support and extend the mattress's service life.
【Comfortable Sleep & Pressure Relief】Our mattress features a unique wavy cotton layer that creates an advanced airflow system. This design allows air to circulate freely, helping to regulate body temperature and keep you cool throughout the night. The wavy cotton also contours to your body, providing exceptional pressure relief and reducing discomfort. Enjoy a deeper, more restful sleep.
【Mattress in a Box】 Our mattress arrives in a compact box, requiring no assembly. The smaller box size also reduces our carbon footprint, making it both convenient and environmentally friendly.
Materials + Care
The comfort layer uses high-density foam that closely conforms to the body's curves; the support layer features individually pocketed springs for precise support and silent, undisturbed sleep. Covered with a breathable, antibacterial, and skin-friendly fabric, and used with a mattress protector and rotated regularly, it will maintain its cleanliness and optimal support for a long time.
