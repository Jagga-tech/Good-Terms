FAQ
Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
Welcome to the Olio Hakizimana LLC Help Center!
We're here to make your shopping experience simple and convenient. Below you'll find answers to some of the most frequently asked questions about our products, orders, shipping, returns, and customer support.
Orders & Payments
Q: How do I place an order?
A: Simply browse our online store, select the products you would like to purchase, and add them to your cart. When you're ready, proceed to checkout and follow the instructions to complete your order.
Q: What payment methods do you accept?
A: We accept the payment methods displayed at checkout. Available payment options may vary depending on your location and the payment services supported by our store.
Q: Can I change or cancel my order?
A: If you need to modify or cancel an order, please contact us as soon as possible at palenikkristin@hotmail.com. We will make reasonable efforts to accommodate your request before shipment. Once an order has shipped, changes or cancellations may no longer be possible.
Q: What should I do if I entered the wrong shipping address?
A: Please contact us immediately with your order number and the correct address. We will try to update your shipping information before your order is dispatched, but changes cannot be guaranteed after processing or shipment.
Shipping & Delivery
Q: Where do you ship?
A: We currently ship within the United States only. International shipping is not available at this time.
Q: How long does shipping take?
A: Orders are typically processed within 1–2 business days, excluding weekends and public holidays.
After shipment, estimated delivery takes 3–7 business days.
The estimated total delivery time is 4–9 business days. Delivery times are estimates and may vary due to carrier delays, weather conditions, or other circumstances.
Q: How much does shipping cost?
A: Shipping costs are calculated at checkout based on your delivery address, selected shipping method, and order details. You can review the applicable shipping charges before completing your purchase.
Q: How can I track my order?
A: Tracking information will be provided when available after your order has shipped. If you need assistance checking your shipment status, please contact our customer support team.
Q: What if my package is delayed or missing?
A: If your package has not arrived within the estimated delivery timeframe, please contact us with your order number. We will help review the shipment status and determine the appropriate next steps.
Returns & Refunds
Q: What is your return policy?
A: We offer a 30-day return policy for eligible products. You may request a return within 30 days of receiving your order, subject to the conditions outlined in our Return & Refund Policy.
Q: How do I request a return?
A: Please email palenikkristin@hotmail.com with your order number, product details, and reason for the return. Our customer support team will review your request and provide return instructions.
Please do not send products back before receiving return instructions.
Q: Who pays for return shipping?
A: For change-of-mind returns or incorrect product selections, customers are generally responsible for return shipping costs.
For confirmed defective, damaged, or incorrectly shipped items, we will cover reasonable return shipping costs when a return is required.
Q: How long does it take to receive a refund?
A: Once your return has been received, inspected, and approved, refunds are generally processed within 5–10 business days to your original payment method.
Your bank or payment provider may require additional time to reflect the refund.
Q: What if I receive a damaged or defective product?
A: Please contact us as soon as possible and provide your order number, a description of the issue, and photographs if available. We will review the situation and assist you with an appropriate resolution.
Products & Compatibility
Q: What types of products do you sell?
A: Olio Hakizimana LLC offers practical electronic products and accessories, including outlet plug adapters, headphones, speakers, and other everyday electronics.
Q: How do I know if an outlet plug adapter is compatible with my device?
A: Please review the product specifications, including plug type, voltage rating, current rating, and supported devices, before purchasing.
Please note that a plug adapter does not necessarily convert electrical voltage. Always confirm that your device supports the power supply requirements before use.
Q: Are your headphones and speakers compatible with all devices?
A: Compatibility depends on the specific product and your device's available connections, operating system, and supported features.
We recommend reviewing the product description and technical specifications before placing an order.
If you're unsure about compatibility, feel free to contact us for assistance.
Q: Where can I find product specifications?
A: Product features, specifications, and available compatibility information can be found on the individual product pages.
If you need additional information, please contact our customer support team.
Q: What should I do if my electronic product is not working properly?
A: First, review any included product instructions and confirm that the product is being used with compatible equipment.
If the issue continues, contact us with your order number and a description of the problem. We will help review the issue and discuss the available support options.
Customer Support
Q: How can I contact customer support?
A: You can contact Olio Hakizimana LLC using the following information:
Company Name: Olio Hakizimana LLC

Q: What information should I include when contacting support?
A: To help us assist you efficiently, please include your order number (if applicable), product name, and a brief description of your question or issue.
For damaged or defective products, photographs may also be helpful.
