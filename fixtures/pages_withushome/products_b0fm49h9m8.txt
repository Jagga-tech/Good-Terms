About This
【Pocketed Springs & Support Foam】Say goodbye to waking up with back pain! Puzdaz 10-inch hybrid mattress combines 700+ individually pocketed springs with 2-inch cooling gel memory foam to give you both strong support and cloud-like comfort. The springs move independently to keep your spine aligned all night, while the memory foam gently hugs your hips and shoulders to relieve pressure points. Perfect for back and side sleepers who want no more morning stiffness!
【Breathable Mattress】The surface of Puzdaz hybrid mattress twin features a 3D honeycomb structure design, covered with 1000+ breathable holes, ensuring super smooth air circulation—no more sweaty back during summer sleep. The reinforced edges eliminate any worries about sagging.
【CertiPUR-US & OEKO-TEX Certification】Puzdaz hybrid mattress twin have passed CertiPUR-US and OEKO-TEX certification. The materials have been strictly tested and are absence of heavy metals and bad substances to ensure the health and safety of users. You can sleep comfortably on a fresh, non-odorous mattress
【Durable Spring Mattress】For optimal performance and comfort, Puzdaz twin mattress requires 48-72 hours recovery period to achieve full expansion and proper firmness. The product ships compressed in a durable, easy-to-handle box for convenient transportation. The compact packaging ensures effortless delivery through narrow hallways and doorways. Upon unboxing, simply place the mattress on your bed frame and allow 3 days for complete air circulation and material stabilization.
【Noiseless & Motion-Isolating】Not just for beds! Fits dorms, guest rooms—anywhere you need comfy sleep! The noise-free coils won't squeak when you move. Plus, the motion isolation means no more waking up when your partner tosses and turns. Sleep like a baby, anywhere!
