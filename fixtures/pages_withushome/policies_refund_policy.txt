Return & Refund Policy
Returns
We offer a 14-day return policy, which means you have 14 calendar days after receiving your item to request a return.
To be eligible for a return, your item must be in the same condition that you received it, unused or unworn, with tags (if applicable), and in its original packaging. You will also need a receipt or proof of purchase.
To start a return, please contact us at cs26shop@gmail.com within 14 days of receiving your item.
If your return is accepted, we will provide a return shipping label, along with instructions on how and where to send your package.
Items returned without prior authorization will not be accepted.
For any return-related questions, please contact us at cs26shop@gmail.com.
Important Notice – Change-of-Mind Returns
For cancellations or returns due to a change of mind, the following charges are non-refundable, where permitted by applicable law:
Original shipping costs
Payment processing fees (2.9% + $0.30 per transaction)
These amounts will be deducted from the refund where legally permitted.
For defective, damaged, or incorrect items, we will provide a full refund, including applicable original shipping costs, without deductions.
Damages and Issues
Please inspect your order upon delivery and contact us immediately if your item is defective, damaged, or incorrect.
We will review the issue and provide an appropriate resolution, such as a replacement or full refund.
Exceptions / Non-Returnable Items
Certain items cannot be returned, including:
Perishable goods, such as food, flowers, or plants
Custom or personalized products
Personal care and beauty products, where applicable
Hazardous materials, flammable liquids, or gases
Sale items and gift cards are generally non-returnable, except where required by applicable law.
Please contact us if you have questions about a specific item.
Exchanges
If you wish to exchange an item, please contact us within 14 days of delivery.
The fastest way to receive the item you want is to return the original item through our return process and place a separate order once the return has been approved.
European Union 14-Day Cooling-Off Period
For eligible orders delivered to the European Union, you have the right to cancel or return your order within 14 days of receiving the goods, for any reason and without justification, in accordance with applicable EU consumer protection laws.
To exercise this right, please contact us at cs26shop@gmail.com within the 14-day period.
Returns, reimbursement terms, and any applicable exceptions will be handled in accordance with EU consumer protection laws.
Refunds
We will notify you once we have received and inspected your returned item and let you know whether your refund has been approved.
If approved, your refund will be processed to your original payment method within 10 business days, or sooner if required by applicable law.
Please note that your bank or credit card company may require additional time to process and post the refund.
If more than 15 business days have passed since your refund was approved, please contact us at cs26shop@gmail.com.
