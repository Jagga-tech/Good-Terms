Shipping Policy
Shipping & Delivery (U.S. Only)
We currently ship within the United States only.
Processing Time
Orders are typically processed within 1–3 business days (Monday–Friday).
During peak periods, processing may take up to 3–5 business days.
Estimated Delivery Time (Transit)
Once your order ships, delivery typically takes 5–10 business days depending on your location and carrier performance.
Total Estimated Delivery Time:
6–13 business days (processing + transit)
Please note: These are estimates and not guaranteed delivery dates.
Order Tracking
Once your order ships, you will receive a shipping confirmation email with your tracking information.
Tracking updates may take 24–72 hours to appear after you receive the tracking number.
Shipping Rates
Standard Shipping: $4.90 (or calculated at checkout)
Free Shipping: Available on orders over $100
Address Accuracy
Please make sure your shipping address is complete and accurate at checkout.
We are not responsible for orders delivered to an incorrect address provided by the customer. If an order is returned to us due to an incorrect address, we can reship it at the customer’s expense or issue a refund minus shipping costs (if applicable).
P.O. Boxes & APO/FPO
P.O. Boxes: [Not supported]
APO/FPO: [Not supported]
Delays (Carrier / Weather / Holidays)
Delivery delays can occur due to weather, high shipping volume, carrier backlogs, or other events outside our control. If your tracking has not updated for several days, please contact us and we’ll help investigate.
Missing / Lost Packages
If your tracking shows “delivered” but you cannot locate your package:
Please check around your property and with household members/neighbors first.
Then contact us within 7 days of the delivery scan at support@homehavenstoreshop.com so we can assist.
If a package is confirmed lost in transit, we will work with you to provide a replacement or an appropriate resolution.
Damaged Items
If your order arrives damaged, please email us within 48 hours of delivery with:
Your order number
Photos of the damaged item(s) and packaging
We will review and make it right.
Order Changes / Cancellations
If you need to change or cancel your order, please contact us as soon as possible.
Once an order has been processed or shipped, we may not be able to make changes.
Contact
For any shipping questions, please contact us at:
support@homehavenstoreshop.com
