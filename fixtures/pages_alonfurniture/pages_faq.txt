Frequently Asked Questions
Welcome to Alon Furniture! Below are answers to the most common questions our customers ask.
What is the shipping cost?
Shipping is 100% free anywhere in the continental United States. No hidden fees or extra charges.
What makes your prices so affordable?
We’ve been selling furniture online since 2016 and are now one of the largest retailers in the industry. With multiple distribution centers and high shipping volumes, we pass our savings directly to you.
Do you ship internationally?
Currently, we only offer shipping within the U.S.
Free shipping to the continental U.S.
Additional charges apply for shipments to Hawaii, Alaska, and Puerto Rico.
International customers may arrange for a U.S.-based freight forwarder, and we’ll ship to them at no cost.
What delivery method is used for my order?
Larger furniture items include free delivery & in-home assembly by trained specialists.
Smaller items (e.g., lamps, accessories) are delivered via UPS or FedEx to your front door.
How long does delivery take?
Most items ship within 1–4 business days.
Delivery time is typically 1–4 weeks, depending on your location and the item’s availability.
We will provide you with a shipping estimate and send regular updates via email and phone.
Are all your products new?
Yes! All of our products are brand new, unused, and in original condition.
When will I be charged?
A temporary authorization may be placed on your card at checkout to confirm funds. You will be fully charged once your order is processed.
Will I be charged sales tax?
Sales tax is applied only in states where required by law. These currently include: AL, AZ, CA, CT, IA, IL, IN, KY, LA, MD, MA, MS, MN, NJ, NV, NC, OH, OK, PA, NY, TN, UT, WA, WI.
Can you ship to APO/FPO addresses?
Unfortunately, we do not currently ship to APO or FPO addresses.
Will I receive tracking information?
Yes! As soon as your order ships, you will receive a tracking number by email. For large items shipped via freight, the carrier will contact you to schedule a delivery time.
What should I do if my order arrives damaged?
If an item is damaged during delivery:
Inspect all items upon delivery.
Note any damage on the delivery receipt.
Save the original packaging.
Contact us within 24 hours at info@alonfurniture.com or call us.
We'll arrange a free replacement, part repair, or professional service—whichever is fastest and most appropriate. If damage is not reported within 24 hours, we cannot guarantee resolution.
What is your return policy?
We accept returns under the following conditions:
Items must be new, unused, and unassembled in the original packaging.
Returns must be requested within 5 days of delivery.
A 15% restocking fee and round-trip shipping charges apply.
Items that cannot be returned include:
Assembled furniture
Custom fabric or color selections
Mattresses, bedding, linens, or power packs
Please contact us first for an RMA (Return Merchandise Authorization) before returning any item.
What is your cancellation policy?
Orders can be canceled within 24 hours of placement with no fees.
If the order has already shipped or is in transit, cancellation will be treated as a return, subject to a 15% restocking fee and round-trip shipping costs.
Cancellation requests must be sent by email with the subject line "Cancellation" and must be confirmed by Alon Furniture to be valid.
Can I refuse delivery if I change my mind?
Refusing a shipment will be treated as a return. You will be responsible for return shipping costs and a 15% restocking fee. If you wish to cancel or return your item, please contact us first for proper instructions.
Do you offer warranties on your products?
Yes! Most of our furniture comes with a manufacturer’s warranty. If there is a defect, we’ll assist with the warranty process or offer a replacement/repair depending on the issue.
Do you offer assembly services?
Yes, for larger items, we offer free in-home assembly as part of your order. Smaller items do not require assembly or will come with instructions.
What happens if I miss a delivery appointment?
Missed appointments may result in rescheduling fees. Please notify us in advance if you need to change your delivery window.
