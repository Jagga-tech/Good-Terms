Warranty Program
At Alon Furniture, we stand behind the enduring craftsmanship of the luxurious pieces we curate. Many of the items available in our premier collection come with comprehensive manufacturer warranties, and our dedicated design support team is here to facilitate these claims seamlessly on your behalf. With over 200,000 expertly selected products, warranty specifics may vary depending on the brand and piece. This policy outlines our streamlined process for submitting and resolving a warranty claim through Alon Furniture.
Initiate Your Claim
Begin by contacting our support team. You will be guided to provide a completed claim form and clear photos of the concern.
Manufacturer Review
We promptly submit your claim to the manufacturer or artisan, who determines if the issue qualifies as a defect under their limited warranty.
Resolution & Support
Approved claims are expertly resolved with replacement parts, professional repair reimbursement, or a pristine new product. Our team guides you at every step.
How to Submit a Warranty Claim
When you contact us regarding a warranty inquiry, our team will begin processing your claim the moment we receive the required documentation. To initiate a seamless warranty claim, please provide the following:
1. A Completed Warranty Claim Form
This form is included in the Warranty Ticket email sent to you when you open your claim with our team.
2. Clear Photos of the Item and the Issue
A high-resolution photo of the product label showing the serial number.
Close-up images clearly showing the defect or issue.
Wide-angle images showing the entire piece of furniture and the affected area in its environment.
Manufacturer Decisions & Next Steps
Once we submit your claim to the manufacturer, they will rigorously assess whether the issue qualifies as a manufacturer defect under their limited warranty. If your claim is approved, one of the following premium resolutions will apply:
Replacement Parts
Once the necessary replacement components are available, we will arrange for them to be shipped directly to you.
Repair Reimbursement
The manufacturer may allocate an amount toward a professional repair. Once you provide a receipt from a certified repair artisan or company, we will promptly reimburse the approved amount.
Important: Any repair costs must be pre-approved in writing by Alon Furniture. Unapproved costs or do-it-yourself repairs will not be reimbursed, regardless of the repair outcome.
Replacement Product
In the event a repair is not possible, a full replacement of the affected piece will be coordinated, complete with our standard delivery care.
General Warranty Guidelines
Warranty coverage and claim approvals are determined solely by the product manufacturer.
Alon Furniture acts as a dedicated intermediary and does not provide direct warranties unless explicitly stated on the product page.
Warranty periods and specific coverage vary by brand and product type—please consult the product's listing or contact our support team for specific terms before or after your purchase.
We're Here to Help
If you have questions about your product's specific warranty or need assistance starting a claim, please contact our support team at Support@AlonFurniture.com. We are here to guide you through a stress-free process.
