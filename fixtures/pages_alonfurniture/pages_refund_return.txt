Refund & Return
At Alon Furniture, your absolute satisfaction is our highest priority. We understand that curating your sanctuary means finding the perfect fit. That is why we offer a refined, clear, and seamless return and refund policy to ensure your complete peace of mind.
30-Day Return Window
You may request a return within 30 days of receiving your order, provided the piece is in its original, pristine condition.
How to Start a Return
Simply email our dedicated support team to receive your return authorization and seamless logistical instructions.
Damaged or Incorrect?
Contact us immediately upon white-glove delivery. We will promptly resolve the issue with an expedited replacement or full refund.
Our Return Process
Eligibility for a Return
To be eligible for a return:
The item must be in its original condition—unused, free from wear or damage, and in its original packaging.
Proof of purchase (e.g., order confirmation or receipt) is required to initiate the process.
How to Start a Return
To initiate a return, contact our design support team. If your return is approved, we will provide:
A return shipping label or logistics coordination for large furniture pickups.
Detailed instructions for safely sending back your item.
Important: Items sent without prior return authorization will not be accepted.
Refund Processing
Once we receive and expertly inspect your return at our facility, we will notify you of the refund approval status. If approved:
A refund will be issued to your original payment method.
Please allow a few business days for your bank or credit card provider to process the refund.
Exclusions & Exchanges
Non-Returnable Items
Certain items are excluded from our return policy to maintain our pristine quality standards:
Custom-built or personalized furniture pieces.
Fabric swatches and material samples.
Clearance, final sale items, or gift cards.
If you are unsure about your item's return eligibility, please feel free to contact our design team before making a purchase.
Exchanges
If you wish to exchange an item for a different finish or style, the quickest method is to return your original item and place a new order once the return is accepted. This ensures fast processing and immediate availability.
Questions or Support?
Our dedicated customer support and design team is here to help. For any return or refund inquiries, please do not hesitate to reach out to our team at Support@AlonFurniture.com.
