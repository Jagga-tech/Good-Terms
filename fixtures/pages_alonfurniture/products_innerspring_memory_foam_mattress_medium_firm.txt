Description
Sleep better every night with this medium firm innerspring memory foam mattress, available in 6-inch and 8-inch profiles across Twin, Twin XL, Full, and Queen sizes. Built with CertiPUR-US certified foams and OEKO-TEX Standard 100 certified textiles, this mattress delivers lasting comfort, proper spinal alignment, and reliable pressure relief for all sleep positions.
Key Features
CertiPUR-US Certified Foam: Rigorously tested for durability, performance, and indoor air quality — free from harmful chemicals.
OEKO-TEX Standard 100 Textiles: Cover fabric certified safe for skin contact, ensuring a healthy sleep surface.
Medium Firm Feel: Balanced firmness provides both cushioning comfort and firm support for proper spinal alignment and pressure point relief.
Mattress-in-a-Box: Compressed and rolled into a single box for easy shipping and setup. Unbox, unroll, and allow up to 72 hours for full expansion to original shape.
Easy Setup: Expands under normal room temperature conditions. In colder climates, allow additional acclimation time.
