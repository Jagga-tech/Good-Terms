# Skill: Check store terms before buying (v2)

Use this whenever the buyer asks you to recommend, compare or buy from online stores.

## The buyer's deal-breakers

1. Opened returns: the item can be returned after it has been opened and used.
2. Free return shipping: the buyer pays nothing to send it back.
3. Sale-item returns: an item bought at a sale price can still be returned.

## Read all of this at every store before deciding anything

a. The product page, including its FAQ, badges and any trial or returns text.
b. The store's return policy at both `/policies/refund-policy` and `/pages/refund-policy`, or the returns link in the footer. Stores often publish two pages that differ. If they disagree, report both and go by the stricter one.
c. Any page linked from the product page or the site menu about a sale, deals, returns, FAQ, trial or warranty. Open the links you actually find instead of guessing addresses. If one of those pages links to another sale, returns, FAQ or trial page, read that one too.
d. Whether the item sits in a clearance, final-sale or sale collection, when the policy treats those differently.
e. If you can ask the store a question directly, ask once. Label the answer as the store's statement. A store-question tool sends nothing from the buyer's own accounts.

If a page will not load or does not exist, say so and carry on. Hold back a recommendation only if you could not read any return policy for that store.

## Report each deal-breaker at each store as exactly one of

- CONFIRMS: a store page says yes. Give the quote and the link.
- REJECTS: a store page says the buyer cannot do it, or must pay. For example, "unused and in original packaging" rejects opened returns. Give the quote and the link.
- DOESN'T SAY: you read everything above and found nothing that covers the buyer's case. List what you read.

A missing answer is not a yes and not a no. If the page covers a different case, such as a free label for unopened items, and is silent on the buyer's case, that is DOESN'T SAY. Amounts deducted from a refund are not return shipping; report them separately. Do not mark CONFIRMS from a trial badge, a store's reputation or your own knowledge. Mention every condition attached to a CONFIRMS.

## Then

- Recommend only stores where every deal-breaker is CONFIRMS.
- Do not prepare a checkout at a store with a REJECTS.
- If the best option has a DOESN'T SAY, ask the buyer before going further.
